Connect to ESPN

Your league is private, so ESPN needs two cookies to let us read it. Everything stays on this Mac in config.json — nothing is uploaded.

1. In Chrome, open your league page on espn.com and log in.
2. Press ⌥⌘I → Application tab → Cookies → https://www.espn.com.
3. Copy the Value of espn_s2 and of SWID.
4. Your league ID is the leagueId= number in your league URL.
